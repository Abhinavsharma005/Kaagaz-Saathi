```markdown
🚨 Over 30% of government scholarship and pension applications in India are rejected for minor document mismatches — a one-letter typo between Aadhaar and a bank passbook, or an income certificate that expired 2 months ago.

For a first-generation student or rural applicant, this means months of delays, repeated Tehsildar visits, or missed academic deadlines.

To solve this, I built **Kaagaz Saathi (कागज़ साथी)** — a voice-first, mobile document pre-checker built for "The Great Indian AI Internship Challenge" by @Gnani.ai.

💡 **How it works in 30 seconds:**
1. 🎙️ **Voice Q&A (Gnani Prisma + Timbre):** The applicant answers questions by voice in Hindi or English. Gnani Timbre speaks natural Hindi prompts and reads back confirmations; Gnani Prisma transcribes noisy microphone input with high phonetic accuracy.
2. 📸 **Document Inspection:** Snaps photos of Aadhaar, Income Certificate, Marksheet, and Passbook. OpenCV deskews, denoises, and checks for blur; OCR extracts fields.
3. ⚖️ **Deterministic Rules Engine ("No Quote, No Pass"):** Compares names across Devanagari and Latin scripts (`indic-transliteration` + `rapidfuzz`), verifies certificate validity dates, and checks income ceilings.
4. 🔊 **Spoken Actionable Verdict:** Timbre reads out exactly what will cause rejection and how to fix it before submission.

🛡️ **Trust Principles:**
- No LLMs, zero hallucinations — 100% deterministic code.
- Every flag shows the exact quoted text from the document.
- Zero data retention: processed in ephemeral memory, permanently purged on reset.

Targeting the **Solo Ninja**, **Real Deal**, and **Main Character** awards!
```
